INSTITUTIONAL FORENSIC PROTOCOL
A Structured Process From Evidence to Recovery
Every case begins with the available facts. The objective is to establish the transaction history, identify relevant parties and determine which recovery mechanisms may be available.
01 — Case Evaluation & Evidence Review
We examine transaction records, payment confirmations, account documentation, wallet information, communications and other available evidence to establish the circumstances of the loss.
02 — Transaction Tracing & Asset Mapping
Where appropriate, financial transactions and blockchain activity are analysed to reconstruct the movement of funds and identify relevant accounts, wallets, exchanges, intermediaries or other entities.
03 — Recovery & Legal Procedures
Where the evidence supports further action, the appropriate procedures may involve financial institutions, payment providers, counterparties, regulatory channels or qualified legal professionals, depending on the case and jurisdiction.
04 — Resolution & Fund Return
Where recovery is achieved through an applicable procedure, the return or settlement of funds is completed through the appropriate verified process. Outcomes depend on the individual circumstances of each case and cannot be guaranteed.
